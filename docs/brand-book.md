# Nugget — Brand Book (v1 · Locked Direction)

> *Find the moment worth keeping.*

The complete system: positioning, logo rules, color, typography, voice, and how it all shows up in the product, on exported clips, and in the app icon.

---

## 1. Positioning

### Who this is for, and what it says
> For creators sitting on hours of raw recordings, **Nugget** is the clip tool that finds the one moment worth posting — and takes it the rest of the way, from raw footage to a captioned, reframed, on-brand clip — built by someone who actually uses it on their own channel.

### The Three Core Pillars
1. **Not another clipper**: Most competitors stop at "find + caption." Nugget's Live Monitor and Studio (reframe, hook, trim, logo, grade, banner, dubbing) go further — the brand says so plainly, not by implication.
2. **Made by a user, not a team**: Credibility comes from the founder's own channel growth, not funding or corporate logos. Voice stays first-person and specific rather than corporate.
3. **Visually its own thing**: The category has converged on one look. Nugget's clay, hand-pressed identity is a deliberate exit from that, not decoration.

## 3. Color Palette: A Clay Palette

A tactile clay palette, deliberately richer than a single terracotta-on-cream combo.

### Core Swatches
- **Coral** (`#E8654A`): Primary action color — buttons, the mark, the "clip this" moment.
  - *Coral 300*: `#F2A392`
  - *Coral 700*: `#B84631`
- **Moss** (`#5E7F5E`): Reserved for success and "kept" states.
  - *Moss 300*: `#9BB89B`
- **Gold** (`#DE9A34`): The scarcest color, saved for the virality/score gauge so it reads as a signal, not decoration.
  - *Gold 300*: `#F0C685`
- **Putty** (`#EFE6D8`): Base interface canvas and raised clay surfaces.
  - *Putty 700*: `#DCCFBC`
- **Ink** (`#2A211C`): Primary charcoal text and dark app surfaces.

### Screen Distribution
- **60% Putty / Ink**: Carry the interface base.
- **30% Coral**: Primary actions and brand highlights.
- **10% Moss / Gold**: Status and virality signals.

## 5. Voice & Tone

> **"A working creator, not a growth team."**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| *"Here's the moment most likely to hook someone."* Specific, confident about what the score means. | *"Unlock viral growth with AI-powered content intelligence."* No buzzwords standing in for a real claim. |
| Call features what the product calls them — Live Monitor, Studio, Highlights. | Rename features to sound fancier in marketing than they're called in the app. |
| Write errors and empty states in plain terms: what happened, what to do next. | Apologize or add filler (*"Oops! Something went a little wrong!"*) — just say what's true. |

### Copy Across Contexts
- **Empty state**: *"Upload something and Nugget will find the moment."*
- **Processing**: *"Scoring your recording — this usually takes a few minutes."*
- **Error**: *"That upload didn't finish. Try again, or check your connection."*
- **Export**: *"Clip saved. Ready to post."*
- **Marketing headline**: *"Stop scrubbing. Start posting."*
